# Google Play screenshot plan

Capture these screens from the finished app on an Android phone. Use real app screens and genuine example investigations; do not fabricate results or imply guaranteed identification, value or fit.

1. **Start an investigation** — show the opening screen and photo action.
2. **Add useful views** — show a clear object photo plus its label, model number or connector close-up.
3. **Review the result** — show likely object, purpose, condition, estimated second-hand value and visual confidence together.
4. **Check a fit or part** — use a watch strap or vehicle-part result with the model/fitment caution visible.
5. **Continue the investigation** — show either follow-up chat or the editable sale-listing draft.

Keep screenshots uncluttered, use brief captions only when needed, and keep the important app content visible on phone-sized screens. Capture each after the relevant feature is available in the build being prepared for release.
