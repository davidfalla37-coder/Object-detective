Object Detective Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Object Detective allows users to photograph or select images of objects and request an AI-assisted investigation to help identify them and provide related information.

Information we process

Images that you choose to submit are sent to our server over HTTPS and processed to provide the requested investigation. If you use follow-up chat, your question and the investigation details needed to answer it are also sent over HTTPS. The server passes the relevant request to an AI service provider over HTTPS. The app does not require an account and does not save submitted images or chat transcripts to an account or cloud investigation history. Investigation history, your appearance preference, and whether you have completed the first-use guide are stored locally on your device.

Camera and photos

Object Detective may request access to your device camera so that you can photograph an object. The app may also allow you to select an existing image from your device. These permissions are used only to provide features that you choose to use.

AI processing

Images and related information are sent securely using HTTPS to the services needed to analyse an object and return an investigation result. These include our hosting provider (Supabase) and an AI processing provider (OpenAI). The app does not use these services to create a user account or cloud investigation history.

Do not submit sensitive personal information, confidential documents, financial information, passwords, identification documents, or other material that you do not want processed.

How information is used

Information submitted through Object Detective is used to operate the app, process object investigations, return results, maintain functionality, improve reliability, and protect the service from misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate Object Detective, provide AI processing, hosting, storage, security, or other technical functionality. The app does not automatically send your feedback vote or feedback text. If you choose to share feedback, the app offers the text to the sharing service you select; no investigation photos are attached.

Retention and deletion

Investigation history and preferences saved on your device can be deleted using the Clear control in Recent investigations, or by clearing Object Detective's app storage or uninstalling the app. Object Detective does not provide user accounts or cloud investigation history. Submitted investigation and chat requests are not saved as application state by the AI Responses API request used by Object Detective. OpenAI states that Responses API abuse-monitoring logs may retain request content for up to 30 days by default; exceptions may apply for safety or legal reasons and provider settings. Hosting providers may also retain limited technical logs under their own retention policies. To ask about a privacy or deletion request, contact the developer using the contact details on the Object Detective Google Play listing. We do not keep a cloud investigation history, and a request to us cannot override a provider's independent retention obligations or policies.

Children

Object Detective is not intended to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. Users should not submit photographs containing personal or sensitive information about children.

Security

Data sent between the app and our server, and between our server and AI processing providers, is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the service. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Safety

Object Detective uses AI-assisted analysis and its results may be inaccurate. Results are estimates and should not be treated as professional advice or proof of identity, authenticity, compatibility, condition, or value.

Vehicle, motorcycle, electrical, structural, medical, gas, pressure, safety-critical, or other potentially hazardous items should be verified with an appropriately qualified source before purchase, installation, repair, or use.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Object Detective's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy requests, or deletion requests, please contact the developer using the contact details provided on the Object Detective Google Play listing.
