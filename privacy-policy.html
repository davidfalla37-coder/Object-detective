Object Detective Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Object Detective lets you photograph or select an object image and request an AI-assisted identification and investigation.

Information we process

When you submit an investigation, the image you selected is sent for processing. If you use follow-up chat, your question and the investigation details needed to answer it are sent too. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information, confidential documents, passwords, payment details, or identity documents.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera or photo you choose to provide an investigation. For some gallery images, it makes a temporary converted copy in private app storage rather than the public Pictures folder. These temporary copies are cleared when the app closes or starts again. The app does not upload photos in the background or access your photo library for unrelated purposes.

Secure anonymous session and usage limits

To protect the analysis service, the app creates an anonymous Supabase Auth account when you first request an investigation or use chat. It does not ask for your name, email address, or password. Supabase issues a random user ID and session tokens; the app stores the tokens in its local WebView storage. The service uses the ID to authenticate requests and apply daily limits of 30 investigations and 120 follow-up messages per anonymous account. These limits may change to protect the service.

Usage counters are kept by Supabase and removed after three days. Anonymous Auth accounts remain with Supabase until you delete the session in the app or request deletion. We do not use these IDs for advertising or link them to an identity ourselves.

Service providers and processing

Object Detective uses Supabase to authenticate the anonymous session, receive and route investigation requests through an Edge Function, and apply usage limits. The request includes the submitted image or follow-up text and relevant investigation context. Supabase's invocation records can include request and response bodies. The project's current Free plan normally makes logs available for one day; Supabase may shorten log availability to one hour if its log-query allowance is exceeded. These logs support service operation, troubleshooting, and security. We do not use them to build advertising profiles.

Supabase routes the request to OpenAI's Responses API to produce and verify investigation results and answer follow-up questions. The API requests are configured not to retain Responses API application state. OpenAI's default abuse-monitoring logs may still contain prompts, responses, or related content and are retained for up to 30 days, subject to approved account-level retention controls and legal or security exceptions.

We do not sell your information. Supabase and OpenAI process information to provide the app's requested features and operate their services.

Information stored on your device

Investigation history, including result details and a preview image, is saved locally on your device so you can revisit it. You can remove individual investigations or clear your history in the app. Chat messages are kept in memory for the current screen session and are not saved to an Object Detective cloud history. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app also removes locally stored history and session tokens.

Retention and deletion

Tap Delete app data and secure session in the app to delete the anonymous Supabase account and its usage counters and clear saved investigations on that device. You can also request deletion from outside the app using the instructions at Object Detective account deletion. If you email us, include the session ID copied from the app so we can find the anonymous account. Supabase invocation logs and OpenAI abuse-monitoring logs are provider operational logs; an in-app deletion cannot remove an individual request from those logs. Provider retention controls and legal or security exceptions apply.

For a privacy question or deletion request, contact objectdetectiveapp@gmail.com.

Children

Object Detective is not intended to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit photographs containing sensitive information about children.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app and its service providers. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

Accuracy and safety

AI-assisted results can be inaccurate and are estimates, not proof of identity, authenticity, compatibility, condition, or value. Verify important details independently.

For vehicle, electrical, structural, medical, gas, pressure, safety-critical, or other potentially hazardous items, consult an appropriately qualified source before purchase, installation, repair, or use.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version and update date will appear on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, privacy requests, or deletion requests, email objectdetectiveapp@gmail.com.
