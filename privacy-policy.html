Object Detective Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Object Detective lets you photograph objects and request an AI-assisted investigation.

Information we process

Photos you submit are processed to provide an investigation. If accounts and cloud history are enabled, investigation records may also be stored so you can view your history.

AI processing

Images may be sent to our configured AI processor to analyse the object. Do not submit sensitive personal information, documents, financial information or other material you do not want processed.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Infrastructure providers may process data only as needed to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

Where cloud history is enabled, users should have a deletion control. Local history can be cleared by clearing app site data.

Safety

AI results are estimates. Verify vehicle, electrical, structural, medical, gas, pressure and other safety-critical items with qualified sources before use or purchase.
