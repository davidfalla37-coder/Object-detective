Object Detective Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Object Detective allows users to photograph or select images of objects and request an AI-assisted investigation to help identify them and provide related information.

Information we process

Images that you choose to submit are sent to our server and configured AI processor to provide the requested investigation. If you use follow-up chat, your question and the investigation details needed to answer it are also sent. Saved investigation history is stored locally on your device; chat transcripts remain only for the current screen session and are not saved to an account or cloud history.

Camera and photos

Object Detective may request access to your device camera so that you can photograph an object. The app may also allow you to select an existing image from your device. These permissions are used only to provide features that you choose to use.

AI processing

Images and related information are sent to our server over HTTPS and forwarded to the configured AI processor over HTTPS to analyse the object. Our server does not save submitted investigations in a database or cloud history. Requests to the OpenAI Responses API set response storage to off. OpenAI states that its standard API abuse-monitoring logs may contain customer content and are retained for up to 30 days by default, unless longer retention is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect its services or others. OpenAI states API data is not used to train or improve its models unless the customer explicitly opts in.

Do not submit sensitive personal information, confidential documents, financial information, passwords, identification documents, or other material that you do not want processed.

How information is used

Information submitted through Object Detective is used to operate the app, process object investigations, return results, maintain functionality, improve reliability, and protect the service from misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate Object Detective, provide AI processing, hosting, storage, security, or other technical functionality.

Retention and deletion

You can delete an individual saved investigation using its × button or remove all saved investigations with Delete all in Recent investigations. This removes the app's saved local copy; it does not delete the original photo from your device's Gallery. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app also removes the locally saved history. We do not keep submitted investigations in our database or cloud history, and response storage is off for AI requests. The AI provider's standard abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days by default, subject to the exceptions above.

Children

Object Detective is not intended to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. Users should not submit photographs containing personal or sensitive information about children.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the service. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Safety

Object Detective uses AI-assisted analysis and its results may be inaccurate. Results are estimates and should not be treated as professional advice or proof of identity, authenticity, compatibility, condition, or value.

Vehicle, motorcycle, electrical, structural, medical, gas, pressure, safety-critical, or other potentially hazardous items should be verified with an appropriately qualified source before purchase, installation, repair, or use.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Object Detective's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy requests, or deletion requests, please contact the developer using the contact details provided on the Object Detective Google Play listing.
