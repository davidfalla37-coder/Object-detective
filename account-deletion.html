Delete an Object Detective account

Object Detective creates a private anonymous Supabase session to authenticate requests and apply usage limits. It does not ask for your name, email address, or password.

Delete from the app

On the app home screen, choose Delete app data and secure session, then confirm. This deletes the anonymous service account and its usage-limit records, and clears saved investigations on that device.

Request deletion if you cannot open the app

Open the app and tap Copy session ID, then email it to objectdetectiveapp@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Object Detective account”. We need that ID to locate an anonymous account because it is not connected to your email address. If you cannot access the app or retrieve the ID, explain that in your email so we can advise you.

Deleting your service account does not remove investigation history stored on your phone. Clear that in the app before deleting, or clear the app's storage in Android settings.
