# Object Detective — Google Play release package

Included: polished PWA, generated promotional image integrated into the home screen, Play listing copy, privacy policy draft, Data Safety draft, Supabase/OpenAI backend source, and Android WebView wrapper source.

A Play Store submission still requires your own developer account, verified identity/contact details, release signing, an Android App Bundle (.aab), the public privacy-policy URL, and the Play Console declarations/testing required for your account.

The Android project targets SDK 35 and uses package `com.objectdetective.app`. Build it in Android Studio/Gradle and configure release signing before upload.
