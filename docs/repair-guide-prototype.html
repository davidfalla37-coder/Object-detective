Example fitting steps

  1. Park safely, switch off the ignition and check the vehicle handbook for the wiper service position.
  2. Lift the wiper arm carefully and protect the windscreen with a folded cloth.
  3. Release the old blade using its connector tab. Do not force the arm or connector.
  4. Attach the replacement using the blade maker’s instructions; check that the lock clicks into place.
  5. Lower the arm gently and test the wiper with washer fluid before driving.
Stop if the connector differs, the arm is damaged, or the handbook instructions do not match. A spring-loaded arm can strike and crack the windscreen.
